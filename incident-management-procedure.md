# Incident Management Procedure

**Process owner:** Operations Lead  
**Applies to:** Service interruptions, fulfilment failures, customer-impacting defects and operational exceptions.

## 1. Purpose
Ensure incidents are recorded, prioritised, assigned, communicated and resolved consistently, with learning captured to reduce recurrence.

## 2. Incident categories
- Service availability or system issue.
- Order fulfilment failure.
- Customer-impacting data or account issue.
- Vendor or rider operational disruption.
- Payment or reconciliation exception.
- Safety, security or privacy concern.

## 3. Severity guide
| Severity | Description | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 – Critical | Broad service outage, serious safety/security/privacy concern or major business interruption | Immediate escalation to accountable leadership and relevant specialist team |
| Sev 2 – High | Significant customer impact or key workflow unavailable | Urgent owner assignment and frequent updates |
| Sev 3 – Medium | Limited impact; workaround available | Prioritised resolution within agreed service window |
| Sev 4 – Low | Minor issue or request with limited impact | Queue and resolve through normal workflow |

Severity must be assigned based on impact and urgency, not the seniority of the person reporting it.

## 4. Procedure
1. **Log:** Create an incident record with time, reporter, affected service/order, symptoms and evidence.
2. **Triage:** Confirm impact, scope, severity and whether specialist escalation is required.
3. **Assign:** Name one incident owner and supporting teams.
4. **Contain:** Apply an approved workaround or containment action where safe.
5. **Communicate:** Provide factual updates to affected stakeholders at an agreed cadence.
6. **Resolve:** Implement and verify the fix or approved resolution.
7. **Close:** Record resolution, timestamps, affected users/orders and closure confirmation.
8. **Review:** For material or recurring incidents, complete a root-cause review and preventive action plan.

## 5. Incident record fields
Incident ID, date/time detected, reporter, category, severity, description, impact, affected order/service, owner, actions, communications, resolution, date/time resolved and lessons learned.

## 6. Safeguards
Do not expose customer data in public channels. Do not make unauthorised financial commitments or bypass approved security, privacy or safety procedures. Escalate suspected security/privacy incidents through the designated specialist route immediately.
