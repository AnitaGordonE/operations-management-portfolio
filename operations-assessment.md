# Operations Assessment — Food Delivery Service

**Status:** Portfolio case study | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Business context
A fictional food delivery business coordinates customers, food vendors, delivery riders and customer support. As order volume grows, the business needs consistent processes and reliable performance information.

## 2. Problem statement
Potential operational pain points include inconsistent vendor response, late order preparation, unclear rider handovers, order inaccuracies, limited status visibility and fragmented complaint handling.

These are hypotheses for the case study, not findings from a real audit. A real assessment would validate them through observation, interviews, process data and customer feedback.

## 3. Assessment objectives
- Map the end-to-end order journey.
- Identify process delays, rework and unclear handoffs.
- Define standard operating procedures and ownership.
- Establish measurable service and quality indicators.
- Prioritise improvements based on impact and effort.

## 4. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Operational interest |
|---|---|
| Customers | Accurate orders and reliable delivery |
| Vendors | Clear order information and predictable workflow |
| Riders | Timely pickup and accurate delivery details |
| Customer Support | Issue visibility and resolution guidance |
| Operations Team | Consistent execution and performance monitoring |
| Management | Service quality, cost control and sustainable growth |

## 5. Current-state discovery questions
- How is an order received and acknowledged?
- How long does vendor acceptance typically take?
- How are preparation delays communicated?
- When and how is a rider assigned?
- How are incorrect, missing or damaged orders handled?
- Where are cancellations and refunds recorded?
- Which team owns each stage and each exception?

## 6. Baseline data to collect
Capture order volume, vendor acceptance time, preparation time, rider assignment time, delivery time, cancellations, order errors, complaints, resolution time and customer feedback. Segment results by vendor, location, day and time where appropriate.

## 7. Improvement objectives
1. Establish one documented order workflow.
2. Assign accountable owners to every handoff.
3. Introduce an exception and escalation process.
4. Create a consistent operational data log.
5. Review performance and corrective actions on a defined cadence.

## 8. Recommended approach
Observe and map the current process, validate root causes, prioritise improvements, pilot the revised workflow with a limited group, review results against a baseline and scale only when evidence supports the change.
