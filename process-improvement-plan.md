# Process Improvement Plan

**Process:** Food order fulfilment | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Improvement objective
Reduce avoidable delays, errors and communication gaps across the order fulfilment journey while maintaining a reliable customer experience.

## 2. Potential root causes to validate
| Observed symptom (hypothesis) | Possible cause | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Slow vendor acknowledgement | No response-time standard or alert | Order timestamps and vendor interviews |
| Late preparation | Capacity constraints or inaccurate preparation estimates | Preparation time by vendor and time period |
| Rider waiting at pickup | Dispatch begins too early or readiness status is unreliable | Rider arrival and order-ready timestamps |
| Order errors | Manual transcription or unclear item notes | Complaint categories and order audit |
| Slow complaint resolution | Unclear ownership or fragmented records | Ticket timestamps and escalation history |

## 3. Prioritised improvement actions
| Action | Expected benefit | Effort | Owner role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Define vendor acknowledgement standard | Faster confirmation and earlier exception visibility | Low | Vendor Operations |
| Introduce standard order status definitions | Better shared visibility | Low | Operations |
| Add delay and unavailability reason codes | More useful operational data | Medium | Product / Operations |
| Trigger rider assignment based on readiness | Reduce unnecessary rider waiting | Medium | Dispatch Lead |
| Use one issue log and escalation path | Faster, traceable resolution | Low | Customer Support Lead |
| Review vendor performance regularly | Targeted coaching and improvement | Medium | Vendor Operations |

## 4. Pilot plan
1. Select a small, representative vendor group.
2. Record baseline performance before changes.
3. Train relevant staff and vendors on the revised workflow.
4. Pilot for an agreed period.
5. Compare results with the baseline and review unintended effects.
6. Adjust the process and obtain approval before wider rollout.

## 5. Measurement
Track vendor acknowledgement time, preparation time, on-time delivery rate, order error rate, cancellation rate and complaint resolution time. Define the measurement window and exclusions consistently.

## 6. Governance
Assign one accountable owner to every action. Review progress weekly. Escalate overdue actions or risks that affect customer safety, data protection, service continuity or approved policy.
