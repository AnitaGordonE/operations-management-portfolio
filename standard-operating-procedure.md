# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Order Fulfilment

**SOP ID:** OPS-SOP-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Process owner:** Operations Lead  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or after a material process change  
**Status:** Portfolio example

## 1. Purpose
To provide a consistent process for receiving, confirming, preparing, dispatching and closing customer orders.

## 2. Scope
Applies to customer support, vendor operations, dispatch/rider coordination and the operations team from order receipt to delivery completion.

## 3. Roles
- **Customer Support:** Handles customer enquiries and records reported issues.
- **Vendor:** Accepts and prepares the order, and flags delays or unavailability.
- **Dispatch Coordinator:** Assigns a rider and coordinates pickup.
- **Rider:** Confirms pickup and delivery status.
- **Operations Lead:** Monitors exceptions, resolves escalations and reviews performance.

## 4. Procedure
1. **Order received:** Confirm that the order appears in the approved order-management system.
2. **Order validation:** Check customer details, items, quantities, delivery address and payment status according to policy.
3. **Vendor acknowledgement:** Send the order to the vendor and record acceptance or rejection.
4. **Exception handling:** If the vendor does not respond within the agreed service window, contact the vendor and escalate according to the escalation matrix.
5. **Preparation:** Vendor prepares the order and updates readiness status. Any delay or unavailable item must be recorded and communicated.
6. **Rider assignment:** Dispatch coordinator assigns an available rider and shares verified pickup and delivery details.
7. **Pickup confirmation:** Rider confirms collection; discrepancies are recorded before departure.
8. **Delivery:** Rider completes delivery and updates the order status.
9. **Order closure:** Confirm completion in the system and record any complaint, refund request or exception.
10. **Daily reconciliation:** Operations reviews incomplete, cancelled, disputed and exception orders.

## 5. Service exceptions
- **Vendor rejection/unavailability:** Notify customer support; follow approved substitution or cancellation policy.
- **Preparation delay:** Record reason, revised estimate and customer communication.
- **Rider unavailable:** Reassign and notify the dispatch coordinator.
- **Incorrect or missing item:** Log the issue, preserve order details and follow the approved resolution policy.
- **Failed delivery:** Record the reason and follow the approved contact and redelivery/refund procedure.

## 6. Records
Maintain order ID, timestamps, vendor, rider, status changes, exception category, communications, resolution and responsible owner.

## 7. Controls
- Do not mark an order delivered without confirmation under the approved process.
- Do not promise refunds, substitutions or compensation outside authorised policy.
- Protect customer personal information and restrict access to authorised personnel.
- Record material exceptions and handovers in the designated system.
