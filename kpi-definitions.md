# Operations KPI Definitions

> Targets should be set only after a reliable baseline, business priorities and service commitments are understood. The workbook contains blank fields for actual data; no performance results are assumed.

| KPI | Definition / formula | Frequency | Suggested owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order fulfilment time | Delivered timestamp minus order received timestamp | Daily / weekly | Operations |
| Vendor acknowledgement time | Vendor accepted timestamp minus order received timestamp | Daily / weekly | Vendor Operations |
| On-time delivery rate | Orders delivered within promised window ÷ eligible delivered orders × 100 | Daily / weekly | Dispatch |
| Order error rate | Orders with confirmed item/quantity errors ÷ completed orders × 100 | Weekly | Vendor Operations |
| Cancellation rate | Cancelled orders ÷ total valid orders × 100 | Daily / weekly | Operations |
| Complaint rate | Orders with a recorded complaint ÷ completed orders × 100 | Weekly | Customer Support |
| First response time | First support response timestamp minus ticket received timestamp | Daily / weekly | Customer Support |
| Resolution time | Resolved timestamp minus ticket received timestamp | Weekly | Customer Support |
| SOP compliance | Audited cases meeting required SOP steps ÷ cases audited × 100 | Monthly | Operations Lead |
| Action closure rate | Actions closed by due date ÷ actions due in period × 100 | Weekly | Operations Lead |

## Measurement rules
- Define the eligible order population and exclusions before reporting.
- Use consistent timestamps and time zones.
- Report numerator and denominator alongside percentages.
- Segment performance where useful (vendor, location, time band).
- Do not compare periods with materially different definitions.
- Investigate outliers and data quality before drawing conclusions.

## Review cadence
Daily operational monitoring for service exceptions; weekly review for trends and corrective actions; monthly review for management decisions and process changes.
